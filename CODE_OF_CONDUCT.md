# Code of conduct

UXorscient welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and
experience levels. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests,
reviews and other project spaces, including contributions prepared with agents.

## Expectations

- Discuss the work with respect. Critique claims, designs and code rather than
  attacking the person presenting them.
- Be clear about observations, assumptions and uncertainty. Provide reproducible
  evidence and acknowledge corrections.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish personal information, credentials
  or private project material without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or
  repeatedly target contributors, or use project spaces for spam.
- Follow maintainer decisions about scope and moderation. Disagreement is welcome
  when it remains specific and respectful.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer, [xormania](https://github.com/xormania), handles conduct concerns.
If an existing private contact route with the maintainer is available, use it.
Otherwise, open an issue titled **Request private maintainer contact** without
details of the incident or identifying information about other people. The
maintainer can arrange a private reporting route before details are shared.
Do not use a public issue to disclose the report. Vulnerability reports instead
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may request a correction, remove or edit inappropriate content,
restrict participation, or block an account according to the conduct and its
impact. Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as
needed to assess and address the concern. A person affected by a moderation
decision may ask the maintainer to reconsider through the same private route.
